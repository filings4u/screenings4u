Built For Employers

Separate the requirements from the workflow.

DOT testing and employer-authority testing are not the same program. Your business should clearly define which testing is federally required and which testing is conducted under company authority.

DOT Program

For covered transportation employees.

  • Applicable DOT testing rules
  • Required policy and education
  • Supervisor training
  • Random testing
  • Required recordkeeping

Company Program

Additional testing under employer authority.

  • Written company policy
  • Applicable state/local law review
  • Clear distinction from DOT testing
  • Consistent employee communications
  • Separate documentation where required
