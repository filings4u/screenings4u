Understanding the Regulation

Part 382 is the FMCSA drug and alcohol testing rule for covered CDL drivers.

FMCSA 49 CFR Part 382 applies to employers and drivers who are subject to the commercial driver's license requirements and perform safety-sensitive functions. It works together with 49 CFR Part 40, which establishes the procedures used for DOT drug and alcohol testing.

The rule covers more than random testing. A compliant program can include pre-employment controlled-substances testing, random testing, post-accident testing, reasonable-suspicion testing, return-to-duty testing, follow-up testing, recordkeeping and related program responsibilities.

Core Part 382 program responsibilities

  • Maintain a compliant DOT drug and alcohol testing program for covered drivers.
  • Keep eligible drivers in the appropriate random testing pool.
  • Ensure random selections are unannounced and reasonably spread throughout the year.
  • Coordinate required DOT testing using applicable Part 40 procedures.
  • Maintain required testing and program documentation.
  • Complete applicable FMCSA Clearinghouse responsibilities.
Who Needs a Consortium?

Built for owner-operators and transportation employers.

FMCSA treats single-driver owner-operators differently from larger employers when it comes to managing the random testing pool.

Owner-Operators

Single-driver companies

FMCSA guidance states that an owner-operator who is not leased to another motor carrier and has only one CDL driver must belong to a consortium for random testing purposes.

  • Consortium random pool participation
  • Selection notification and coordination
  • Enrollment documentation
  • Testing and recordkeeping support
Motor Carriers & Fleets

Multi-driver employers

Employers with more than one covered CDL driver may manage their own random program or use a consortium/third-party administrator to reduce the administrative burden.

  • Driver roster management
  • Random selection administration
  • Testing coordination for selected drivers
  • Centralized compliance records

Current FMCSA Random Testing Rates

Annual minimum testing rates for FMCSA-regulated drivers.

FMCSA currently identifies a 50% annual minimum rate for controlled substances testing and a 10% annual minimum rate for alcohol testing. Random selections must be unannounced and reasonably spread throughout the calendar year. DOT agencies can change rates, so employers should confirm the current rate each year.
