Accessibility Statement

screenings4u is committed to providing digital services that are accessible to people with disabilities.

Updated September 1, 2026
On this pageCommitmentOur standardAccessibility featuresKnown limitationsAssistanceFeedback

1. Our commitment

We work to make our public websites, customer portals, employer tools, and training experiences usable by the widest possible audience. Accessibility is an ongoing process involving design, development, content, testing, and support.

2. Accessibility standard

Our goal is substantial conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, where reasonably applicable. This goal does not mean that every page or third-party component currently satisfies every criterion.

3. Measures we take

  • Semantic headings, labels, landmarks, and keyboard-operable controls.
  • Visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Error messages and instructions designed to be understandable.
  • Periodic automated and manual review of core customer journeys.
  • Accessibility consideration when selecting or updating vendors and embedded tools.

4. Third-party content and known limitations

Some payment, mapping, laboratory, video, document, authentication, or other third-party experiences are controlled by their providers. We work to identify practical alternatives when an external component creates a barrier.

PDFs, historical documents, or specialized testing forms may not be fully optimized for every assistive technology. Contact us for assistance or an alternative format.

5. Assistance and reasonable accommodations

If a digital barrier prevents you from ordering, scheduling, accessing results, completing training, or using another service, contact us. We will work with you to provide the information or service through a reasonable alternative method.

6. Feedback

Email support@screenings4u.com, call (773) 245-7009, or write to screenings4u, 8537 S Pulaski Rd, Chicago, IL 60652. Please identify the page, the problem encountered, the assistive technology or browser used if relevant, and your preferred contact method.
