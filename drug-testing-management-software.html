Simple Daily Workflow

Customers manage the program. screenings4u manages the platform.

The customer side is focused on the tasks employers need every day. Behind it, the screenings4u management portal controls accounts, plans, permissions, testing services, random programs, results, documents and support.

  • Customers log in to their secure company account.
  • They manage employees and place testing orders.
  • Random selections and testing requirements appear as actionable items.
  • Results and documents are released to authorized users.
  • screenings4u staff can manage the entire lifecycle from the administrative portal.

Two Connected Portals

One platform for customers. One control center for screenings4u.

The software will be built around two role-specific experiences sharing the same underlying account, service and compliance data.

Customer Portal

Employers, fleets and consortium customers get a focused workspace for the parts of the testing program they need to manage.

  • Testing orders and donor passes

  • Results, certificates and documents
