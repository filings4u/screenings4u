Course Curriculum

Ten units covering the fundamentals of DOT specimen collection.

The curriculum follows the major areas identified by screenings4u for becoming familiar with DOT urine specimen collection procedures and related responsibilities.

01

Introduction & Background

DOT regulations, regulated agencies, safety-sensitive positions, reasons for testing, drug testing panels, acceptable testing methods and approved laboratories.

02

The Collector

Who may become a collector, collector training requirements, collector and employer responsibilities, and restrictions placed on collectors.

03

Collection Site & Supplies

Collection-site requirements, donor privacy, site security, required collection supplies and collection-kit contents.

04

Custody & Control Form

DOT release, waiver and privacy forms, the CCF, required information, completion steps and proper distribution.

05

The Collection Process

Testing considerations, collection steps, donor instructions, specimen inspection, splitting and preparing specimens for laboratory shipment.

06

Monitored & Direct Observation

Monitored collections, who may perform them, required procedures and documentation, plus direct observation requirements.

07

Shy Bladder

Shy bladder procedures including starting without delay, proper donor instructions, required steps and documentation.

08

Problem Specimens

How to respond to temperature-out-of-range, adulterated and substituted specimens and properly document the collection.

09

Unusual Collections & Mishaps

Refusals, missing signatures or initials, catheter situations, fatal and correctable flaws, torn seals and Public Interest Exclusion topics.

10

Non-Regulated Testing

Key differences between DOT and non-DOT collections and conducting DOT and non-DOT collections on the same donor.

11

Starter Guide

Access to a DOT Specimen Collector Starter Guide and supporting reference materials.

12

Policy Guides

Reference materials include sample DOT and NON-DOT Drug & Alcohol Policy guides.
