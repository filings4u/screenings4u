Post-Accident Emergency Testing

24/7/365 rapid on-site mobile response for DOT and non-DOT post-incident drug and alcohol screening requirements.

Protect Your Liability with Timely Screenings

Following a workplace accident, the clock starts ticking immediately. Federal DOT rules mandate alcohol testing within 2 hours (and no later than 8 hours) and drug testing within 32 hours of an incident. Our rapid dispatch network reduces company legal risk, fulfills workers' compensation rules, and brings clarity when you need it most.

When is a DOT Post-Accident Test Required?

For commercial motor vehicles (FMCSA), testing is required immediately if the incident involves:

Any accident involving a human fatality (Always mandatory)
Bodily injury requiring immediate medical treatment away from the scene + Driver receives a citation
One or more vehicles sustain disabling damage requiring a tow truck + Driver receives a citation
