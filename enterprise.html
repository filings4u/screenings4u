What is Screenings4U?

Screenings4U is an enterprise drug and alcohol testing platform that centralizes workforce testing, compliance workflows, results, reporting, and program administration across every location and business unit.

Does Screenings4U support separate DOT and non-DOT testing programs?

Yes. The platform maps distinct, non-overlapping workflow parameters for federally mandated DOT rules and highly customizable employer-defined non-DOT policy panels.

Can we manage infrastructure permissions across hundreds of locations?

Yes. The underlying multi-tier organizational tree scales easily across Parent corporations, subsidiaries, custom logistical hubs, and departments using granular, role-based database constraints.
