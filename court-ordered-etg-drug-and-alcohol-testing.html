Before You Order

Use the exact testing requirement you were given.

Court and legal testing requirements can vary. If your paperwork specifies a panel, testing method, frequency, or reporting requirement, use that information when choosing your service.

  • Review the test type and panel
  • Confirm the required testing frequency
  • Bring required identification and paperwork
  • Follow any reporting instructions from the ordering party
IMPORTANT

Not sure which test was ordered?

Do not guess. Send us the testing requirement or tell us what your court, probation officer, attorney, or monitoring program requested and our team can help you identify the correct service.
