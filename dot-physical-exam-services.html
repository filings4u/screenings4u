Before Your Exam

Arrive prepared for your DOT physical.

Requirements can vary by driver and medical history. Review the instructions from the exam location and bring the documentation relevant to your situation.

IDENTIFICATION

Bring valid ID

Have your required identification and driver information available at check-in.

MEDICAL INFO

Bring relevant records

Bring medication information, medical documentation, or specialist records if the examiner or your condition requires them.

APPOINTMENT

Confirm the location

Verify your appointment time, participating exam location, and any site-specific instructions before traveling.

FOLLOW-UP

Keep your documentation

Retain the applicable documentation from the examination for your driver and employer records.
