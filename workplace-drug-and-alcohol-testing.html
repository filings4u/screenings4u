Employer FAQ

Questions about workplace testing?

Review the basics before getting started.

Employers of drivers subject to the applicable FMCSA CDL requirements must comply with the DOT drug and alcohol testing rules.
FMCSA identifies pre-employment, reasonable suspicion, post-accident, random, return-to-duty and follow-up testing as applicable testing types.
FMCSA testing requirements apply to covered drivers performing safety-sensitive functions; an employer may have separate company-authority testing policies where permitted.
Yes. FMCSA requires supervisors of covered drivers to receive training on the indicators of alcohol misuse and controlled-substances use.
