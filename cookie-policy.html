1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a browser or device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, software development kits, and identifiers that help websites remember information or understand use.

2. Types of technologies we use

Strictly necessary

Used for security, authentication, session continuity, fraud prevention, checkout, load balancing, and core site features. Disabling these may prevent services from working.

Preferences

Remember choices such as interface settings, cart contents, and communication preferences.

Analytics and performance

Help us understand traffic, errors, page performance, and how features are used. Information may be aggregated or pseudonymous.

Advertising or campaign measurement

If enabled, these technologies measure campaigns or support relevant outreach. They should be activated only as disclosed and with consent where required.

3. Third-party services

Payment, authentication, hosting, analytics, communications, embedded content, and support providers may place or access their own technologies. Their use is governed by their privacy notices. For example, payment providers may use device and fraud-prevention signals during checkout.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through browser settings. Where a consent control is available, use it to change nonessential cookie preferences. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent login, checkout, saved preferences, or other functions.

Browser “Do Not Track” signals are not interpreted consistently across the industry. We respond to legally recognized opt-out preference signals where required and technically supported.

5. Retention and changes

Session cookies expire when a browsing session ends; persistent cookies remain for a defined period or until deleted. Retention depends on purpose and provider. We may update this policy when technologies or legal requirements change.

6. Contact

Questions may be sent to support@screenings4u.com.
