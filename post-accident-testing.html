Post-Accident FAQ

Questions before arranging testing?

Post-accident testing is DOT-regulated drug and/or alcohol testing that may be required after a qualifying accident involving a commercial motor vehicle driver.
Whether testing is required depends on the circumstances of the accident and the applicable DOT regulations. Employers should evaluate the event against the requirements that apply to their operation.
Drug and alcohol testing requirements are separate and depend on the circumstances. screenings4u can help coordinate the applicable service when testing is needed.
Timing can be critical after a qualifying accident. Employers should follow the applicable DOT requirements and begin the testing process as soon as reasonably practicable.
