Privacy Policy

This policy explains how screenings4u handles personal information across our websites, portals, testing, compliance, and training services.

1. Scope

This policy applies when screenings4u controls personal information collected through our public websites, customer and employer portals, communications, orders, training, and service delivery. It does not replace a separate privacy notice, authorization, consent, or regulated disclosure provided for a particular service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact data: name, email, phone, organization, job title, address, credentials, and preferences.
  • Order and payment data: services ordered, transaction status, billing details, receipts, and payment-provider references. Complete card numbers are handled by our payment processor rather than stored by us.
  • Testing and service data: donor identifiers, appointment and collection information, testing reasons, results, forms, chain-of-custody information, employer instructions, and support records.
  • Training data: enrollment, lesson progress, assessments, scores, certificates, and activity.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser, device, timestamps, referring pages, cookie identifiers, and interaction logs.
  • Communications: messages, calls, forms, tickets, and feedback.

We may receive information from you, an employer or authorized representative, collection sites, laboratories, payment processors, identity providers, regulators, and other service providers.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, schedule, process, and support requested services.
  • Verify identity, authorization, eligibility, payment, and account access.
  • Deliver results, donor passes, receipts, notifications, training, and certificates.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, investigate incidents, and enforce agreements.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, audit, recordkeeping, and reporting requirements.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, measure, and improve our services.
  • Send transactional messages and, where permitted, service or marketing communications.

4. When information is disclosed

We may disclose information to authorized employers and representatives; laboratories, Medical Review Officers, collection sites, carriers, trainers, consultants, payment processors, cloud and communications providers; government or regulatory authorities; and professional advisers. We may also disclose information in a business transaction or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or comply with law.

Recipients receive only information reasonably necessary for their role and are subject to contractual or legal obligations where applicable. We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Health, testing, and background-screening information

Testing and screening information may be sensitive and subject to sector-specific rules, authorizations, contracts, and retention requirements. HIPAA does not apply to every testing or employment record. Where screenings4u acts for an employer, that employer may determine the purpose and handling of the information.

Consumer reports and background-screening information, when offered, are handled under applicable agreements and laws, including the Fair Credit Reporting Act where it applies. Individuals should use the dispute and rights process provided with the relevant report or notice.

6. Cookies and online technologies

We use necessary cookies for authentication, security, checkout, preferences, and site operation. We may use analytics technologies to understand performance and usage. See our Cookie Policy for details and controls.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, legal and regulatory obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, audits, and legitimate business records. Different records have different retention periods.

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You may update account information, manage certain communications, or request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. We will verify requests and respond as required by applicable law. Some records cannot be deleted when retention is legally required or necessary to complete a service.

You can manage browser cookies and opt out of nonessential communications using provided controls. We do not knowingly direct services to children under 13.

9. External services and changes

External websites and services have their own privacy practices. We may update this policy as our practices or laws change. Material changes will be posted with a revised effective date and additional notice where required.
