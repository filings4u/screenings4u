A certification does not create a business by itself.

A sustainable drug testing operation needs more than collection knowledge. You need a defined service menu, appropriate training, laboratory and MRO relationships, reliable procedures, business infrastructure, client-facing systems, and a plan for getting employer accounts.

Our launch plans organize those moving parts into one implementation path. Choose the level of support that matches the type of business you want to build.

What a professional launch should address

Build the systems around the service before you begin taking appointments.

  • Choose the testing and collection services you can support reliably.
  • Complete the training and qualifications appropriate for those services.
  • Establish laboratory, MRO, results, and supply workflows.
  • Create customer-facing pricing, scheduling, branding, and communication systems.
  • Document procedures for collections, records, client onboarding, and quality control.
  • Build a realistic employer and local-market outreach plan.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before you buy a launch package.

Use these answers to understand the implementation model and what is—and is not—automatically included.

Do I need to know exactly which drug testing services I will offer before purchasing?

No. The kickoff process helps you define the operating model, but you should have a general idea whether you want to focus on mobile collections, a fixed collection site, employer testing support, or a broader C/TPA model.

Does the package make me DOT-qualified automatically?

No. Training enrollment can be included, but qualification requires successful completion of the applicable training, demonstrations, mock collections, or other requirements tied to the service you plan to perform.

Are state filing fees, licenses, and third-party subscriptions included?

No, unless a specific line item states otherwise. Government filing fees, licenses, laboratory pass-through charges, software subscriptions, registered-agent services, and other third-party costs are separate from the screenings4u implementation fee.

How long does the launch process take?

Timelines vary based on your state, training completion, agency processing, third-party account approvals, and the scope of your package. Many business setup tasks can move within a 30–45 day implementation window, but that is not a guaranteed completion date.

Can I upgrade after purchasing Launch Foundation?

Yes. If your business expands into employer administration, consortium services, or a broader TPA model, our team can review what has already been completed and quote the remaining implementation work.

Can I start with only a lab account or C/TPA setup?

Yes. The individual services remain available if you do not need a complete launch package.
