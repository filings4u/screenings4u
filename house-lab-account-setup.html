House Lab Account FAQs

Common questions about setting up and using a House Lab Account with screenings4u.

A House Lab Account is a professional account designed for laboratories and collection sites. It provides a streamlined way to support drug and alcohol testing operations, supplies, and available results management through a central workflow.
These account options are designed for independent labs, collection sites, and workplace testing providers that support employer clients and other organizations needing professional screening services.
Depending on your selected account option, your setup includes designated CCFs, collection kits, and related shipping supplies to help prepare your operation.
