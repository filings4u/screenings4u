What is not automatic

Approvals, qualifications, and local requirements still matter.

Your requirements depend on your services, location, customers, and whether you perform DOT-regulated work. Training enrollment, filing coordination, or account setup does not guarantee an agency approval, laboratory relationship, license, CLIA status, or professional qualification.

Questions before you launch

Mobile business FAQs.

Use these answers to understand what the launch packages organize and where outside requirements still apply.

Do I need a special vehicle to start?

No specific vehicle is included in the package. Your vehicle and equipment setup should fit the collections you perform, storage needs, local requirements, and client expectations.

Can I serve employers with a mobile testing business?

Yes, mobile collection models commonly focus on employer convenience. Your agreements, availability, collection procedures, and DOT/non-DOT workflows should match the services you are qualified to provide.

Does the package make me a qualified DOT collector?

No. Training enrollment can be included, but qualification depends on successful completion of the applicable training, demonstrations, mock collections, and other requirements for the service.

Are government fees and software subscriptions included?

No, unless the written scope specifically states otherwise. Filing fees, licenses, registered-agent charges, laboratory pass-through charges, vehicles, equipment, and recurring third-party software subscriptions are separate.

Can I start with Starter and add C/TPA services later?

Yes. You can begin with the mobile collection foundation and add consortium, Clearinghouse, employer administration, or broader C/TPA infrastructure as the business grows.

Can I request only certain setup items?

Yes. Use the custom quote form above to select the specific services, systems, supplies, and business setup items you want reviewed.
