Build responsibly

There is no one-size-fits-all mobile testing business.

Your requirements can vary depending on your location, the services you provide, your clients, and the organizations you work with.

Start with a realistic plan for the business you want to build—not a generic checklist.
  • Research local and applicable requirements before launching.
  • Match your training to the collection services you plan to perform.
  • Create procedures that support consistency and professionalism.
  • Understand the expectations of the laboratories and clients you work with.
  • Build your business around services you can support reliably.
Questions before you start

What entrepreneurs commonly want to know.

Your specific path depends on the services and market you choose. These questions can help you begin the conversation.

Do I need training before starting a mobile testing business?

Training needs depend on the services you plan to perform and the requirements associated with those services. It is important to understand the expectations before offering collection services.

Can a mobile business work with employers?

Many mobile testing models are built around providing convenient collection access for employers and other organizations, but your client strategy and service agreements should match your capabilities.

Can I offer more than one type of collection service?

That depends on your training, qualifications, business model, and the requirements associated with each service you intend to provide.

How do laboratory relationships fit into the business?

Laboratory and testing relationships can be an important part of supporting the services you offer. The appropriate setup depends on your operating model and service needs.
