Before Your Visit

Know what to bring before collection.

Requirements can vary by testing service and collection location, so review your instructions before arriving.

  • Bring required photo identification
  • Bring your testing order or authorization
  • Confirm the collection location and hours
  • Follow any service-specific instructions
  • Arrive within the required testing window
IDENTIFICATION

Bring valid ID

Collection sites generally require identification before testing can begin.

AUTHORIZATION

Have your order ready

Bring the testing order, authorization, or instructions associated with your service.

LOCATION

Confirm before traveling

Make sure you have the correct collection site, hours, and any appointment details.

COLLECTION

Follow site instructions

The collector will guide you through the applicable testing and documentation process.
