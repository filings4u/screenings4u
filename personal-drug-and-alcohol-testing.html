Before You Test

Know what to expect before your appointment.

A little preparation can make the collection process easier and help you arrive ready to complete your testing.

Before Your Visit

Have the information and identification you need ready for collection.

  • Know which test you were instructed to complete
  • Bring required identification
  • Bring any testing authorization or order
  • Review the collection location instructions
  • Arrive during your scheduled testing window

At Collection

Follow the collector's instructions and complete the applicable testing process.

  • Check in with the collection facility
  • Present required documentation
  • Follow collection instructions
  • Complete required paperwork
  • Confirm the collection is complete
